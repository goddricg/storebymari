# Engineer Brand and Growth

Build trust and compounding value rather than short-lived manipulation. Ground every claim in product truth and every experiment in consent, privacy, and measurable outcomes.

## Establish context

1. Define the audience, job, category, alternatives, product truth, brand promise, and desired behavior.
2. Inspect current content, journeys, analytics, search visibility, customer evidence, consent, and accessibility.
3. Separate brand strategy, product experience, channel execution, and measurement.
4. Verify current platform, advertising, privacy, and search-engine requirements from primary sources before consequential work.

## Build the brand system

- Define positioning, audience, problem, category, differentiation, reasons to believe, voice, and prohibited claims.
- Align naming, visual identity, typography, color, illustration, motion, and interface behavior with one coherent promise.
- Make copy specific, honest, accessible, and useful at the moment of decision.
- Use storytelling to clarify transformation and evidence; never fabricate customers, urgency, scarcity, or outcomes.
- Design for long-term trust across marketing, onboarding, product, billing, support, and recovery.

## Engineer the growth journey

- Map awareness, consideration, acquisition, activation, value realization, retention, expansion, referral, and reactivation.
- Identify the highest-leverage constraint from evidence rather than optimizing every stage.
- Reduce friction that does not protect users; preserve informed consent, comprehension, security, and cancellation.
- Build community and referral loops around genuine recurring value.
- Treat virality as a measured product mechanism, not a demand to exploit contacts or attention.
- Connect SEO to helpful public content, crawlability, performance, semantic structure, authority, and truthful metadata.

## Experiment responsibly

1. State hypothesis, target segment, primary metric, guardrails, sample assumptions, duration, and stop rule.
2. Instrument exposure and outcomes with consent and minimal data.
3. Protect against novelty, selection, seasonality, attribution, and multiple-testing errors.
4. Prefer causal tests where feasible; label observational evidence.
5. Inspect qualitative failure and accessibility effects alongside conversion.
6. Ship a winner only when the effect is meaningful, durable, and does not harm guardrails.

## Coordinate councils

Use Product, Design, Behavioral UX, Engineering, Data, Security/Privacy, Business, and Customer Success perspectives as needed. Behavioral science may improve comprehension and motivation; never use it for coercion, addiction, discriminatory targeting, or dark patterns.

## Return

Report:

1. audience, positioning, brand promise, and evidence;
2. journey and primary constraint;
3. messaging/content/interface strategy;
4. channel and SEO plan;
5. experiment design, instrumentation, guardrails, and results;
6. privacy, accessibility, trust, and operational risks;
7. next decision and invalidating evidence.

Do not equate traffic, clicks, or a model's copy preference with business value.
