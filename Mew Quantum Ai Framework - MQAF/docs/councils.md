# Mew Multi-Disciplinary Councils

Councils are routed specialist perspectives, not permanent committees. The Orchestrator activates only the minimum set that can change the decision or verification plan.

## Council map

| Council | Members | Activate for |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | Architect, Frontend, Backend, Database, Security, DevOps, AI, Cloud/Networking | Software boundaries, implementation, reliability, operations |
| Business | CEO/Product advisor, Startup/SaaS, Pricing, Monetization, Finance/Accounting/Investment awareness | Business model, economics, prioritization, packaging |
| Psychology | Cognitive and consumer psychology, behavioral science, motivation, decision-making, habit formation | Comprehension, motivation, ethical behavior design |
| Marketing | Brand, SEO, storytelling, copy, community, growth, funnel, ads | Acquisition, positioning, lifecycle, discoverability |
| Design | UI, UX, typography, color, illustration, motion, accessibility, human-interface design | Product experience and design systems |
| Science | Physics, mathematics, statistics, biology, chemistry, neuroscience, complexity science | Research, models, experiments, cross-disciplinary constraints |
| Innovation | First principles, systems/design thinking, TRIZ, Lean Startup, Blue Ocean, game theory | New approaches and constraint removal |
| Human | Negotiation, communication, leadership, teaching, coaching, sales, customer success | Human decisions, learning, alignment, support |
| AI | Prompt engineering, agents, MCP, RAG, vector retrieval, fine-tuning strategy, LLM evaluation | AI product and agent systems |
| Security and Governance | Cybersecurity, privacy, legal awareness, compliance, risk analysis | Rights, trust boundaries, enterprise and regulatory risk |
| Wisdom | Critical, second-order, probabilistic, Bayesian, scientific, game-theoretic and inversion reasoning | Consequential uncertain decisions |

Finance, accounting, investment, psychology, scientific, legal, and compliance perspectives provide analysis and identify professional-review needs. They do not impersonate licensed professionals or claim certainty outside the available evidence.

## Activation protocol

1. Start from the outcome and affected boundaries.
2. Select a primary council that owns the deliverable.
3. Add councils only for a named decision, risk, or verification gap.
4. Give every council the same facts, constraints, authority, and acceptance criteria.
5. Preserve independent initial recommendations for consequential decisions.
6. Route disagreement through `$run-debate-decisions`.
7. Keep one Orchestrator responsible for integration and the final recommendation.
8. Stop when acceptance coverage is complete and further council work has low expected value.

## Council return contract

Every council returns:

```text
Perspective:
Recommendation:
Evidence:
Assumptions and confidence:
Trade-offs and second-order effects:
Failure modes:
Required verification:
Permission or professional-review gates:
```

No council receives authority merely from expertise. Production, destructive, financial, legal, publication, privacy, and external actions remain controlled by the task and permission ledgers.

## Ethical psychology and growth

Use behavioral knowledge to improve comprehension, autonomy, motivation, and recovery. Prohibit deceptive scarcity, hidden costs, obstruction, addiction loops, discriminatory targeting, covert persuasion, or exploiting a person's vulnerability.

## Science and innovation boundary

Use cross-disciplinary knowledge through current primary evidence and qualified review. Do not infer that a metaphor from quantum physics, neuroscience, biology, or another science proves a software or business claim.
