## Verification and response contract

Return:

1. status: complete / partial / blocked;
2. outcome;
3. files or artifacts changed;
4. checks run with exact results and canonical evidence states;
5. evidence references;
6. assumptions, risks, and unresolved blockers;
7. requested integration-manager action.

Also report whether a route fallback occurred, why it occurred, what state was
observed before failover, and whether the verifier was independent from the
author.

Do not claim success without evidence. Do not modify files outside ownership. Escalate a dependency conflict before editing a shared surface.
