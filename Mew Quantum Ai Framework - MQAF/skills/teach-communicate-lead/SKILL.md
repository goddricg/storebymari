# Teach, Communicate, and Lead

Increase understanding and agency. Do not use communication skill to hide uncertainty, pressure consent, or manufacture agreement.

## Establish the human outcome

1. Identify audience, relationship, decision rights, prior knowledge, language, context, stakes, and desired action.
2. Separate what the audience must know, decide, feel safe asking, and be able to do afterward.
3. Infer communication needs cautiously from observable cues; do not diagnose personality or emotion.
4. Name power differences, incentives, conflicts, accessibility needs, and cultural or language constraints.

## Teach

- Start with the learner's goal and existing mental model.
- Explain from concrete example to abstraction and back to application.
- Segment information, define jargon, use analogies carefully, and reveal complexity progressively.
- Ask retrieval, prediction, comparison, and application questions instead of only repeating content.
- Give feedback that is specific, actionable, and separate from identity.
- Verify learning through performance or teach-back, not through polite agreement.

## Communicate and lead

- Lead with outcome, evidence, risk, decision, and next owner.
- Match detail to the audience while keeping facts invariant.
- Make roles, decision rights, dependencies, deadlines, and escalation paths explicit.
- Invite dissent before commitment and summarize the decision after it.
- In conflict, separate shared outcome, observations, interpretations, needs, and requests.
- In coaching, ask questions that improve the person's reasoning; do not create dependence on the coach.

## Negotiate, sell, and support ethically

- Prepare interests, alternatives, constraints, tradeable terms, objective criteria, and walk-away conditions.
- Seek mutual clarity and voluntary agreement; never exploit vulnerability or conceal material terms.
- Use sales discovery to test fit, not to pressure a predetermined purchase.
- Make claims specific and supportable. Disclose limitations, costs, implementation effort, and risk.
- In customer success, connect adoption to the customer's outcome and surface product gaps honestly.
- Preserve privacy, accessibility, cancellation, complaint, and escalation paths.

## Coordinate the Human Council

Invite Communication, Leadership, Teaching, Negotiation, Sales, and Customer Success perspectives only when material. Use Product, Design, Psychology, Legal/Risk, or Security review for messages that affect behavior, rights, sensitive data, money, or safety.

## Return

Provide:

1. audience and desired outcome;
2. key message or learning objective;
3. structured communication, lesson, negotiation, or leadership plan;
4. evidence, examples, and questions;
5. objections, misunderstandings, power, and ethical risks;
6. confirmation method and follow-up.

Do not claim persuasion, learning, alignment, or loyalty without observable evidence.
